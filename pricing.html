Which wearable devices and smart rings are supported? ▼
NovaPulse AI natively supports Apple Watch (Series 4+ and Ultra), Oura Ring (Gen 2 & Gen 3), Whoop 4.0, Garmin (all Forerunner & Fenix models), and Google Pixel Watch via Apple Health and Google Health Connect.
How is my biometric health data secured? ▼
We employ a strict zero-knowledge architecture. All raw telemetry is parsed using WebGPU/CoreML inference on your local physical device. Raw sensor data is never transmitted to or stored on our cloud servers.
Can I cancel or switch subscription tiers anytime? ▼
Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any moment with a single click inside your profile settings. If you cancel during your 14-day free trial, you will not be billed.
How soon will I notice improvements in my energy and focus? ▼
Most users observe measurable improvements within 72 to 96 hours as NovaPulse maps their baseline circadian cortisol peaks and aligns work sessions with natural biological windows.
